## What this paper answers

For a declared tool-call event interface, a declared observation/predicate vocabulary, and a reviewed assume–guarantee contract library: how can one deterministic contract checker make online gate decisions and offline recorded-trajectory verdicts? The paper does **not** prove that the observation channel, policies, natural-language translation, blocked effects, or free-form semantics are correct.
